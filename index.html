About the game

Kovala is a physics puzzle with one simple rule: every ball goes into the bucket of its own colour. Drag to aim, watch the red arrow charge up and let go. Balls arc through the air, roll off rocks and bounce out of the wrong bucket, so every throw needs a plan.

  • 200 levels that get seriously harder; after level 100 the buckets slide and flip.
  • Fewer shots, higher score and up to three stars per level.
  • A new daily challenge every day, the same level for everyone.
